The ROCK Association

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

The ROCK Association ("The ROCK," "we," "us," or "our") is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website (gorock.org), our member portal (hub.gorock.org), and our communications with members, volunteers, and supporters.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, which may include your name, email address, mobile phone number, role within the organization, and other details you share when you join, volunteer, register for events, or contact us. We also collect basic technical information (such as device and usage data) when you use our website or portal.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to operate The ROCK Association and serve our members and volunteers, including to: coordinate events and volunteer activities; send organizational announcements, event sign-up confirmations and reminders, and task assignments; maintain member records; respond to your questions; and comply with legal obligations.

SMS/text messaging program

The ROCK Association offers SMS text notifications to board members and volunteers through our ROCK Hub member portal. Participation is voluntary.

  • Consent. By providing your mobile phone number and opting in through the ROCK Hub member portal, you consent to receive recurring SMS text messages from The ROCK Association, such as announcements, event sign-up confirmations and reminders, and volunteer task assignments.
  • Message frequency. Message frequency varies based on organizational activity.
  • Cost. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
  • Opt-out. You can cancel SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. After you reply STOP, we will send one confirmation message and will not send any further SMS messages unless you opt in again.
  • Help. For help, reply HELP or contact us at info@gorock.org or (407) 750-1198.
  • Carriers. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Mobile information is never shared for marketing. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate our programs and communications (for example, our messaging and email providers), and only as needed to provide those services, or when required by law. As stated above, mobile opt-in and consent data is never shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, to maintain member records, and to comply with our legal obligations. You may request that we update or delete your information as described below.

Your choices

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. You may also contact us to review, update, or delete the personal information we hold about you.

Children's privacy

The ROCK Association serves youth as part of its mission. Where we collect information relating to minors in connection with our programs, we do so with appropriate parental or guardian consent and handle it consistent with applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
