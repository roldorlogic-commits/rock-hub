SMS Text Message Opt-In

How board members and volunteers consent to receive text messages from The ROCK Association.

The ROCK Association, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, sends SMS text notifications to its board members and volunteers — including organizational announcements, event sign-up confirmations and reminders, and volunteer task assignments. Participation is voluntary and requires opt-in.

How members opt in

  1. A member joins The ROCK Association and creates their profile in the ROCK Hub member portal (hub.gorock.org).
  2. In their Notification Preferences, the member provides their mobile number and actively checks the box(es) for the SMS notifications they wish to receive. No boxes are pre-checked — consent is opt-in only.
  3. By checking the SMS consent box, the member agrees to receive recurring text messages at the number they provided.
The form below is a public copy of the SMS opt-in exactly as it appears to members inside the ROCK Hub portal. It is shown here so the opt-in flow and consent language can be reviewed without a member login. All checkboxes are unchecked by default; members must actively opt in.
